⚠️ Payment is fully automated. The amounts are booked directly through our payment service provider.

## Transactions by Debit & Credit Card

When a paid transaction takes place at an eCarUp station, the corresponding amount (based on the price set by the station owner) is charged to the debit or credit card stored by the driver directly after the charging session. The funds are transferred to an account of the station owner in a fully automated way.

Transactions under CHF 0.50 are not processed by the system.

The following debit & credit cards are accepted: Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, Diners Club and JCB

### Payment

To pay for charging sessions with the eCarUp app, users store a debit or credit card in the app under Account. The amount for each charging session is charged to the stored card directly after deactivation.

The amount is calculated based on actual consumption (you only pay for what you have charged), according to the price for energy and parking time defined by the station operator.

No further fees apply.

The driver automatically receives a receipt by email directly after the charging session. Occasionally it may end up in the spam folder.

⚠️ Important: The VAT information is stored by the station owner based on their own conditions (private individuals or foundations, for example, are not subject to VAT).

### Were additional fees noted on your credit card statement besides the charging price?

This may affect drivers in the EU area under certain circumstances and must be clarified with the credit card provider. The general terms and conditions of most card providers then contain, for example, the following sentence:

"Payment transactions in which amounts in euros are transferred to an account in an EEA contracting state that is not part of the euro area, and in which the currency conversion is carried out there."

Please contact the card provider in that case.

Learn more about the security of financial transactions in the article [Stripe](/stationsbetreiber/account-verifizieren).
