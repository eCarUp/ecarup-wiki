## Driver notifications

### Failed payment

If a payment with the credit or debit card stored in eCarUp has failed, you will receive a notification.

You can find more information at the following [link](/fahrer/fehlgeschlagene-zahlung).

### Cancelled payment

If a payment with the credit or debit card stored in eCarUp fails and repeated attempts to settle the payment are unsuccessful, you will receive an email. Please contact us at support@ecarup.com.

### Payment method expiring

The stored payment method (credit or debit card) expires in one month. To ensure payments are processed smoothly, the payment information should be updated in the app in good time.

You can find more information at the following [link](/fahrer/ecarup-app).

## Driver statistics

### Monthly overview

The monthly overview is sent automatically to the driver's email address at the beginning of the following month.

⚠️ Important:

1.  At least one paid charging session must have taken place.

2.  We have no influence on the tariffs or on the information about the station operators.

3.  The version is currently only available in English.

4.  The overview is not an invoice.

## Web portal

Log in to our [web portal](https://admin.ecarup.com/authentication/login) with your eCarUp account.

1.  All payment transactions are listed, including the payment status.

2.  Under the menu item "History" you can create your driver statistics.

3.  RFID cards can also be added via the web portal.


![General Information – Figure 3](/img/fahrer-fahrerstatistik/03.png)

Drivers can create their "driver statistics" as follows:

1.  Under the "Driver" tab (Fahrer), click on History (Verlauf).

2.  Select the start and end date and click on the random arrow (Zufallspfeil) to update the list.

3.  Optionally, an export to an Excel file can also be created.


You can find more information on the topic of calibration law and on the verification of charging sessions on the following [wiki page](/sonstige-informationen/eichrecht-in-deutschland).
